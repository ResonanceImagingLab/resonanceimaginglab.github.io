# Resonance Imaging Lab

Work in the lab is focused on magnetic resonance imaging or MRI. We are interested in developing quantitative MRI techniques that will allow us to draw meaningful conclusions from pixel (or voxel) values. You may use a thermometer to gain quantitative information about your health status. We want to be able to the look at the image pixel values from an MRI image to be able to know if the tissue is healthy or not.
